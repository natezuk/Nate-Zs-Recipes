---
title: Sticky toffee pudding
---
*Based on the recipe from [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/ultimate-sticky-toffee-pudding). Makes 12-15 puddings.
### Ingredients:

- 500 g pitted medjool dates
- 350 mL water
- 350 g plain flour
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 eggs
- 175 g butter
- 300 g sugar
- 1/4 cup black treacle
- 200 mL milk

### Method:

1. Boil the water in a saucepan. Once it starts boiling, add the dates and remove from heat. Let sit for at least 30 minutes. Then, blend the dates and water until there are no small chunks. Set aside.
3. In a small bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix until homogeneous.
4. In a larger bowl, beat the butter and sugar until smooth.
5. Add the eggs and continue to beat until fully mixed in. Then add the black treacle and beat until mixed in.
6. Add the flour mixture followed by the milk in 1/2 additions, mixing after each addition.
7. Finally, add the blended dates, and blend into the batter until fully mixed in.
8. Pour the batter into a 9 x 12 in greased baking pan, it should come to at most 2/3 up the sides of the pan.
9. Bake at 175 C for 55 minutes, until a knife stuck into the pudding turns out clean.
10. Let the puddings cool, and start making the toffee sauce.
11. When the puddings are cool (about 30 minutes at least), cut the puddings into 12-15 rectangles. Do this before pouring the toffee sauce over the puddings (*see next section*).
## Toffee sauce

- 350 g dark brown sugar
	- *Can substitute for 350 g sugar and 2 Tbsp black treacle*
- 100 g butter
- 500 mL double cream

### Method:

1. Combine the sugar, butter, and 350 mL of cream in a saucepan. Heat until the butter and sugar are dissolved.
2. Turn the heat to medium high and boil the sugar mixture for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally (the temperature should reach about 105 C).
3. Immediately remove from heat and stir in the rest of the cream.
4. Pour the toffee sauce over the pudding in the tray. The pudding can be served immediately, or you can let it sit to allow the toffee sauce to soak into the pudding, for at least 1 hour.
	- *This is a lot of sauce, but it is meant to soak into the pudding. I ended up using a spatula to push some of the pudding out of the way to allow sauce to get down the sides. I had about 1/2 cup of sauce left over.*
