---
title: Nestle-style Chocolate Chip Cookies
---
*Basically the nestle chocolate chip recipe, but I modified it for grams of ingredients.*

### Ingredients:

- 325 g flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 150 g white sugar
- 150 g dark brown sugar
- 300 g butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- about 1 cup chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)
- 1 tsp cinnamon

### Method:

1. In a large bowl, beat together the butter and sugar. Then add eggs and vanilla and mix until incorporated.
2. In a separate smaller bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt, and mix until evenly distributed.
3. Add the flour mixture to the larger bowl with the butter and sugar in thirds, mixing after each addition.
4. Add the chocolate chips, walnuts, and cinnamon, and mix until evenly distributed in the dough.
5. Scoop large spoonfuls of dough onto a baking tray with parchment paper.
6. Bake at 175 C for 15 minutes.
