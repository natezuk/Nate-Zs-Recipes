## Making the Cookies

### Ingredients:

- 700 g plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 250 g butter (*can also substitute with 150 g oil, but you need to add a bit more flour*)
- 250 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1/4 cup milk or water
- 2 tsp vanilla
- Filling (a recipe for poppy seed filling can be found below)

### Method:

1. In a large bowl, beat together the butter and sugar. Then add the eggs and mix in, followed by the milk and vanilla. Mix until homogeneous.
2. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Mix until evenly distributed.
3. Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture in 1/3 to 1/2 additions, mixing after each addition. Continue to mix until the flour is fully incorporated and a soft dough forms.
4. Remove the dough from the bowl and wrap with plastic wrap. Place in the fridge for about 1 hour.
5. Preheat the oven to 175 C. Remove the dough from the fridge and cut the dough in half. 
6. Starting with the first half, roll the dough to a thickness of about 1/8 inches. Use a medium-sized glass to cut the dough into circles (about 3.5 inches in diameter). Place each circle on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
7. Add a small spoonful of filling to each circle, then fold over the sides of the circle so a triangle forms. Pinch the corners of the triangle and bring them towards the center, so that the filling can be seen in the center but the triangle holds its shape.
8. Repeat steps 6-7 for the rest of the cookies.
9. Bake at 175 C for 20 minutes, until the dough is golden brown.

## Poppy seed filling

### Ingredients:

- 300 g poppy seeds
- 250 g white sugar
- 1 cup water
- Pinch salt
- 1/2 tsp vanilla

### Method:

Combine all of the ingredients in a small pot. Heat on medium and stir continuously until the filling starts to thicken and clumps form while stirring. Remove from heat and let cool until at room temperature.
