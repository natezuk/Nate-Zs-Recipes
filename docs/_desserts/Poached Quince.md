---
title: Poached Quince
---
*When I made this I just ate the quince as a dessert. But I think it would be great with some other dessert food. The sauce is nice too and could be added to something separately.*

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg quince, peeled and cubed
- 1 L water
- 3/4 cups white sugar

### Method:

Bring the water and sugar to a boil. Add the quince and reduce to a simmer. Simmer for at least 1 hour, until the quince is light pink in color, and a fork stuck into the quince easily goes through it.
