---
title: Hermit cookies
---
*From Baking & Pastry from the Culinary Institute of America, except that I skip the fondant topping. These cookies are kind of like soft gingerbread cookies.*
### Ingredients:

- 550 g flour + 9 g baking soda (or just 550 g self-rising flour)
- Pinch salt
- 170 g butter
- 340 g sugar (*or use 340 g dark brown sugar and skip the molasses*)
- 100 g black treacle or molasses
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp allspice

### Method:

1. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium sized bowl and mix together.
2. In a larger bowl, beat the butter and sugar. And the molasses and eggs and mix until fully incorporated and the batter starts to get more liquid (though still sticky). Add the cinnamon and allspice and mix in.
3. Add the flour mixture in 1/2 additions, mixing in well after each addition.
4. Scoop a large spoonful of the batter onto a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Continue for each cookie until it fills the baking tray (with a couple inches in between each).
5. Bake at 190 C for 12 minutes.
