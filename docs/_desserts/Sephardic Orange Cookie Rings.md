---
title: Sephardic Orange Cookie Rings
---
*From "The World of Jewish Desserts". Traditional for the break fast after Yom Kippur.*

### Ingredients

- 750 g plain flour
- 1 Tbsp + 1 tsp baking powder (17 g)
- Pinch (1/8 tsp) salt
- 250 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 cup (4 fl oz) orange juice + sunflower oil (*1 pressed large orange, fill the rest with sunflower oil*)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Egg wash (1 egg + 1 tsp water + 1 tsp sugar)

### Method

1. In a medium sized bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, salt and mix together.
2. In a large bowl, beat the eggs and the sugar. Add the orange juice, oil, and vanilla and mix until it lightens in color. Add the dry ingredients in 1/2 additions and mix until fully incorporated and it forms a soft dough. Place in fridge.
3. Preheat oven to 175 C.
4. Roll out the dough into ropes that are 1/2-inch thick. Cut into 4 to 5-inch lengths. For each, connect the ends so it forms a torus, and roll in your hands to help the ends stick together. Repeat until the baking tray is full.
5. Brush with egg wash, then bake for 20 minutes (until golden brown).
