### Ingredients:

- 2 eggs
- 200 g sugar
- 1/2 cup tahini
- 100 mL vegetable or canola oil
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 2 tsp maple syrup or silan
- 360 g plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder

### Method:

1. In a large bowl, beat together the sugar and oil. Then add the eggs, vanilla, maple syrup/silan, and tahini and mix well.
2. In a smaller bowl, combine the flour and baking powder, and mix until evenly distributed.
3. Add the flour mixture in 1/3 additions to the sugar and oil mixture, stirring after each addition. Let sit in the fridge while the oven is preheating.
4. Place in large spoonfuls on a baking sheet with parchment paper.
5. Bake at 175 C for 15 minutes.
