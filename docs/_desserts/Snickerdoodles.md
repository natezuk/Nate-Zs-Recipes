---
title: Snickerdoodles
---
*Normally you make these by rolling the dough in sugar and cinnamon, but Jasmine prefers it without the layer of sugar.*

### Ingredients:

- 250 g granulated sugar
- 250 g butter
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 450 g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp cream of tartar or lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Method:

1. In a large bowl, beat together the butter and sugar. Then add the eggs, vanilla, and cream of tartar (or lemon juice) and mix until fully incorporated.
2. In a smaller bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt, and mix well.
3. Add the flour mixture to the large bowl in 2-3 additions, mixing after each addition. Mix until the flour is fully incorporated and the mixture becomes a cookie dough that holds together.
4. Scoop the cookie dough onto a baking tray with parchment paper in large spoonfuls.
5. Bake for 15 minutes at 175 C.
