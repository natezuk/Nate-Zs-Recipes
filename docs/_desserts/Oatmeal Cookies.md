### Ingredients:

- 200 g brown sugar

- 1 Tbsp vanilla
- 200 g plain flour
- 100 g whole wheat flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 200 g oats
