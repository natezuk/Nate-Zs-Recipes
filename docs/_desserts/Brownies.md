---
title: Brownies
---
*Based on the recipe from Baking & Pastry.*

### Ingredients:

- 300 g butter
- 300 g dark chocolate
- 6 eggs
- 600 g sugar
- 1/2 Tbsp vanilla
- 250 g all purpose flour
- 75 g cocoa powder
- 200 g chopped walnuts (optional)

### Method:

Preheat the oven to 175 C / 350 F  
1. In a double boiler (e.g. a small pot sitting in simmering water in a larger pot), melt the butter. Then add the chocolate and continuously stir until the chocolate is melted. Remove from heat and set aside.  
2. Beat together eggs, sugar, and vanilla until it lightens in color and most of the sugar is dissolved (5-8 min).
3. Add 1/3 of the egg mixture to the pot with chocolate in small additions to temper the chocolate. Once the chocolate is tempered (feels like it's close to room temperature), add it to the rest of the egg mixture and beat until fully incorporated.
4. Combine flour and cocoa powder in a small bowl, then add to the egg and chocolate mix. Mix in until fully incorporated.
5. Add walnuts and mix in.
6. Pour the batter into a greased 9 x 13 inch pan. It should fill to halfway up the sides of the pan (if it's more than 2/3 of the height of a pan, then you need a larger pan).
7. Bake for 30-35 min. After 30 min, check the brownies. When it's ready, there should be a crust on top, but the center will be _slightly undercooked_ (a knife stuck into the center will come out with some chocolate stuck to it).  
8. Remove from the oven and give it at least 30 min to cool.
