---
title: Flourless Chocolate Cake
---
*I like to use icing to top this cake. The recipes for various icings can be found below.*

### Ingredients:

- 200 g butter
- 200 g chocolate (dark)
- 200 g sugar
- 200 g eggs (3-4 eggs)

### Method:

1. Melt the butter in a double boiler, add chocolate and mix until melted
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs and sugar
3. Gradually add the chocolate mixture to the eggs and sugar (temper it) and mix together until uniform
4. Pour into a 20 cm greased pan
5. Bake at 160 C for 30 minutes, until a knife stuck in it comes out clean.
6. Let the cake cool for at least 1 hour, and place in the fridge to store.

## Raspberry icing

### Ingredients:

- 1/2 small pack of raspberries (50 g?)
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 400 g powdered sugar

### Method:

1. Combine the raspberries and lemon juice in a small bowl, and use a large spoon to mash the raspberries thoroughly.
2. Add the icing sugar in 1/4 additions (about 100 g each), and completely mix in. After all four additions, the icing will loosely hold its shape and run slowly.

## Pomegranate ginger icing

### Ingredients:

- 70 g pomegranate seeds, + 50 g pomegranate seeds for topping  
- about 6 g ginger finely chopped, + 5 g ginger in thin slices for topping  
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- 400 g powdered sugar

### Method:

1. Mash 70 g pomegranate seeds to release the juice, then add the cinnamon and 6 g finely chopped ginger.
2. Gradually add the powdered sugar, mixing with each addition
3. After spreading the frosting over the cake, sprinkle the 50 g pomegranate seeds and 5 g ginger slices covering the cake. *This added more liquid to the top of the frosting and made the frosting look a bit less even, but it still tasted pretty good :)*

## Orange mint icing

### Ingredients:

- 3 fl oz orange juice
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped mint leaves
- 400 g powdered sugar
- Orange zest

### Method:

1. Combine the orange juice and mint. Mix and muddle the mint leaves.
2. Gradually add the powdered sugar, mixing with each addition.
3. Spread the icing over the cake, then sprinkle the orange zest over to icing.
