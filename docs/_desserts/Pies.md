*The first section describes making the dough, which is the same for all of the pies listed below. Each pie filling is described afterwards.*

The instructions below are for 9-inch pie pans.

## Pie crust

### Ingredients:

*Makes 2 crusts*

- 2 cups plain flour (250 g)
- 1 tsp salt
- 250 g butter
- 5 Tbsp cold water

### Method:

1. Combine the flour and salt.
2. Rub the butter into the flour and salt until evenly distributed and there are no large butter chunks in the flour mixture.
3. Add the cold water 1 Tbsp at a time. Mix after each addition until the water fully incorporated. Continue adding water until a cohesive dough is formed and all of the flour is mixed into the dough.
4. Shape the dough into a flattened ball and wrap with plastic wrap. Place in the fridge for at least 1 hour.

## Apple pie

### Ingredients for filling:

- 8 medium apples, peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup light brown sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon

### Method:

Preheat the oven to 175 C.

1. Combine the ingredients in a large bowl and mix until the sugar and cinnamon coats the apples.
2. Roll out half of the pie dough so it is 1-2 cm larger than the pie pan diameter. Place on the bottom of the pie pan.
3. Add the filling. Then cover with the other half of the dough, rolling to a similar size as the first piece. 
4. Pinch around the edges to seal the two pieces of dough together, and remove excess crust. Take a fork and poke holes throughout the top crust of the pie to let air escape.
5. Line the outside of the pie pan with aluminum foil so it is partly covering the outer side of the crust. Bake the pie with the foil covering at 175 C for 20 minutes. Then remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes.
6. Let cool before serving.

## Pumpkin pie

*Only half of the dough is needed for pumpkin pie.*

### Ingredients for filling:

- 1 16 fl oz can of pumpkin
- 2 eggs + 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp allspice
- 12 fl oz fresh cream

### Method:

Preheat the oven to 200 C.

1. Combine the pumpkin, egg, and sugar in a bowl and mix until incorporated. Then add the salt, the spices, and fresh cream, and mix until homogeneous.
2. Roll out half of the pie dough so it is 1-2 cm larger than the pie pan diameter. Place on the bottom of the pie pan. Pinch around the edges to form a pattern around the edge of the pie, and remove excess crust.
3. Add the pie filling.
4. Bake the pie at 200 C for 15 minutes, then reduce to 175 C and bake for 45 minutes.
