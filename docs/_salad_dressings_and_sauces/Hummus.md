---
title: Hummus
---
*This is somewhat based on the Jerusalem cookbook. I think this needs work, but here's my latest version.*

### Ingredients:

- 300 g chickpeas, cooked
- 3 fl oz chickpea water
- 2 fl oz lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Olive oil
- Paprika for seasoning

### Method:

1. Combine all of the ingredients except the olive oil in a blend and blend until homogeneous.
2. Pour into a container and cover with a layer of olive oil. Sprinkle with paprika.
