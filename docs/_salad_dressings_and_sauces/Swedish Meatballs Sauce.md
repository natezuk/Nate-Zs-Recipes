---
title: Swedish Meatballs Sauce
---
*Based on the Ikea recipe found here: https://globalnews.ca/news/6846188/ikea-meatball-recipe-coronavirus/*.

Takes 10-15 minutes to make.

### Ingredients:

-   40 grams of oil
-   40 grams plain flour
-   300 mL warm water
-   150 of thick double cream (*I used 15% cream*)
-   2 teaspoons of soy sauce
-   1 teaspoon of Dijon mustard

### Method:

Cook the meatballs either beforehand or during the sauce..

1. Heat the oil in a small pot on medium heat. When the oil is hot, add the flour (it should bubble at bit immediately after putting it in). Mix the flour until it dissolves in the oil. Cook for a few minutes.
2. Add the water and stir continuously until the sauce thickens, after a few minutes of stirring. It will thicken pretty suddenly, like a thick gravy.
3. Add the cream, soy sauce, and mustard, and mix until incorproated.
4. Add the meatballs and cover with the sauce.
