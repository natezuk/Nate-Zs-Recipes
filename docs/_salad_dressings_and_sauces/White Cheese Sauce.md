---
title: White Cheese Sauce
---
*Good for pasta and gnocchi.*

### Ingredients:

- 4-5 cloves garlic
- 60 g butter (*I thought this also turned substituting with 60 g oil* )
- 1/2 tsp green peppercorns (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp flour
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 cup fresh cream (light cream or about 15% cream)
- 60 g grado padano or parmesan cheese
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley flakes
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper

### Method:

1. Heat the butter in a medium-sized saucepan on medium heat. Add the garlic, salt, and green peppercorns (optional), and sautee until the garlic just starts to brown.
2. Add 1 Tbsp of flour and mix until it is fully incorporporated by the butter and begins to bubble. After about 30 seconds, pour in the water and continue stirring. The mixture will very quickly form a paste.
3. Once the mixture becomes a paste, pour in the fresh cream. Heat for about a minute, then pour in the cheese. Mix until the cheese is completely melted.
4. Add the parsely flakes and ground pepper, and mix for 10-15 seconds. Turn off the heat, then pour the sauce over the cooked pasta.
