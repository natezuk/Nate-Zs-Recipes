---
title: Honey Mustard Dressing
---

### Ingredients:

- 1 fl oz olive oil
- 1 fl oz white wine vinegar
- 2 tsp mustard (strong English mustard is the best)
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 1/4 tsp salt
- Ground pepper

### Method:

Combine all of the ingredients in a container and mix well. Toss with the salad, or pour over to coat.

---
### Notes:

- I originally made this for salad, but it is a great marinade for chicken breast as well. Marinade the chicken breast, then bake for 20 minutes on 200 C, until the chicken breast is cooked through.
