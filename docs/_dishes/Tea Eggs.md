---
title: Tea Eggs
---
*I considerably modified this from a recipe online, but I don't remember where...*

### Ingredients:

- 6 fl oz (or 200 mL) dark soy sauce
- 1250 mL water
- 5 bay leaves
- 1 large cinnamon stick
- 5 hot peppers
- 3 star anise
- 10 cloves
- 1 tsp szechuan peppercorns
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 4 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 Tbsp black tea leaves (or 4-5 black tea bags)
- 12-13 eggs

### Method:

1. Boil the eggs: Boil water, then add the eggs and boil for 12 minutes.
2. While the eggs are cooking, combine all of the other ingredients in a medium-sized pot. Bring to a boil, then turn of the heat and let sit for 10 minutes.
3. Use tongs to remove the large items in the brine, like the tea bags (if used) and the cinnamon stick.
4. Deshell the eggs and place them in the brine. Wait until the brine cools to close to room temperature, then place in the fridge.
5. Let the eggs sit for at least 8 hours, or overnight. When ready, remove the eggs from the brine and place in a separate container (*You can use the brine for other things, or to make another round of tea eggs.*)

---
### Notes:

- Another way of making tea eggs is to just crack the shell throughout and then let the eggs sit in the brine for a longer period of time. This produces a cracked texture on the eggs. However, we thought these weren't as flavorful, so we've preferred the deshelled version described above.
