---
title: Orange Chicken Tofu
---
*Originally this was a recipe that I found to make knock-off Panda-Express-style Orange Chicken. The recipe was nice but not authentic (it didn't taste artificial enough). But I adapted the sauce for breaded tofu instead, which turned out a lot better. Jasmine refuses to call it "Orange Tofu" because it's based on an orange chicken recipe, hence why it is called "Orange Chicken Tofu".*

Makes 4-6 servings. Serve with rice.

## Step 1) Make the sauce

*The sauce needs to sit for a while in the fridge, so that's why it should be done first.*

### Ingredients:

- 2 large oranges, include juice and peels (not the rind, just the peels. *After squeezing the orange, I slice it into segments and remove the peel from the rind in each one.*)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large ginger piece (1 inch in size), minced
- 6 Tbsp dark brown sugar
- 2.5 fl oz soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar

### Method:

1. Add the oranges, cloves and ginger to a small bowl.
2. In another bowl, combine the sugar, soy sauce, and vinegar. Mix until the sugar is dissolved.
3. Add the liquid to the oranges in the small bowl. Mix until combined.
4. Let sit in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.

## Step 2) Breaded tofu

### Ingredients:

- 700 g tofu, cubed into 1-inch pieces
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 200 g plain flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 eggs
- 400 mL water

### Method:

1. Combine the tofu with the soy sauce in a small bowl, in order to marinate the tofu. Cover and place in the fridge. *This can be done while getting the other ingredients ready.*
2. Combine the plain flour, salt, pepper, and paprika in one bowl. In another bowl, beat the eggs, and then add the water and mix until homogeneous.
3. Dredge each piece of tofu:
	- *Have a separate large plate ready*
	1) For each piece of tofu, dip the tofu in the flour mixture and rotate to coat it with flour.
	2) Then dip the tofu into the egg mixture. Use your other hand to rotate it and coat with egg.
	3) Place the tofu on the plate before moving onto the next piece.
	- Repeat steps 1-3 for each piece of tofu *twice*.
	- Then, coat each piece of tofu with the flour mixture (as in 1), and place in large bowl.

## Step 3) Cooking

### Method:

1. Heat vegetable or canola oil in a large pan. There should be enough oil submerge 1/3 of the tofu.
2. Use tongs to place each piece of tofu in the hot oil. Every couple of minutes, rotate the pieces of tofu to cook each side. When the breading is cooked on every side of the tofu, place on a plate covered with a paper towel to absorb the oil. *This step will need to be done in 2-3 rounds to cook the tofu, which likely won't all fit in the pan.*
3. When all of the tofu is cooked, pour the oil out into smaller pan that can withstand the heat of the oil, in order to let the oil cool. Wipe the pan down with a paper towel.
4. Pour the sauce into the pan, and turn the heat to medium. As the sauce is heating, remove each orange peel with tongs.
5. As the sauce is heating, regulary mix it so it is not heating unevenly (you will see it bubbling more in the center than at the edges of the pan). Heat the sauce until most of it is bubbling. At this point, it should have a syrupy consistency, and the sauce will not immediate recoat the pan when being mixed.
6. Turn the heat off. Then add the breaded tofu. Use a spatula to gently flip the tofu, so that it is completely coated with the sauce.
