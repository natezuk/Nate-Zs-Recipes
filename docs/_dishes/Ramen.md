title: Ramen
---
*Based on the recipe from Let's Make Ramen!*
Makes 2 servings.

### Ingredients:

- 500 mL stock
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp mirin
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 100 g ramen noodles
- 2 tea eggs (one egg per bowl, I have a recipe [here](Tea%20Eggs.md))
- Green onions
- Bean sprouts
- White cabbage, finely sliced

### Method:

1. Boil the ramen noodles. 
2. As the noodles are cooking, in another pot heat the stock, soy sauce, mirin, and sesame oil until just boiling.
3. When adding to the bowl, first pour in half the broth in each bowl. Then, drain the noodles, and use tongs to place the noodles in the broth.
4. Cut the tea eggs in half and place yolk-side up on the noodles. Then add the other veggies around the bowl.
