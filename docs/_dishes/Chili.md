---
title: Chili
---
*Based on a recipe from [The Wholesome Dish](https://www.thewholesomedish.com/the-best-classic-chili/).*

### Ingredients:

- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 kg ground beef
- 2 red bell peppers, diced
- 2 medium onions, diced
- 1 Tbsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 4 Tbsp tomato paste
- 3 Tbsp baharat
- 2 Tbsp cumin
- 2 Tbsp hot paprika
- 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 800 g (2 cans) diced tomatoes
- 800 g (2 cans) red kidney beans, drained
- 750 mL water

### Method:

1. In a large pot, heat the oil on medium heat. Then add the peppers, onions, salt, and black pepper, and sautee until they start to become translucent (about 5 minutes).
2. Add the ground beef and use a spatula to break up the beef and mix it to heat throughout. Sautee for 7 minutes until the ground beef is mostly grey from the cooking.
3. Add the tomato paste, baharat, cumin, and paprika, and mix until fully incorporated.
4. Add the sugar, tomatoes, beans, and water, and mix until incorproated. 
5. Then cover with a lid and continue to heat until it is boiling. Reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring occassionally. After 25 minutes, remove the lid, and continue cooking for another 5 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and cover with the lid until it is ready to serve.
