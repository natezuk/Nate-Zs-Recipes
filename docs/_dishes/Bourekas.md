*The puff pastry comes from Baking & Pastry, "Butter puff pastry dough", p. 231. I make the recipe in full and freeze the dough that I don't use. However, the last time I made bourekas, I used nearly all of the dough.*

## Potato bourekas

### Puff pastry:

Puff pastry dough:

1.  Bread flour = 2 lb
2.  All purpose flour = 8 oz
3.  Salt = 1 oz
4.  Butter = 8 oz
5.  Water = 18 fl oz

Puff pastry roll-in:

1.  Butter (pliable) = 2 lb 4 oz
2.  Bread flour = 4 oz

Mix the dough together and shape into a rough rectangle. Wrap and place
in refrigerator for 30-60 min.

Make the roll-in by mixing the butter and flour, first with a sturdy
spatula or utensil then with your hands. Shape into a rectangle, wrap in
plastic wrap, and refrigerate for 30 min.

Turn out the dough on a lightly floured surface and roll out to a
rectangle \> 2x the size of the roll-in. Lock-in the roll-in by sealing
the dough around the roll-in, then apply a 4-fold. Refrigerate for 30-45
min.

Remove from the refrigerator, rotate 90 degrees relative to the original
orientation, roll out and apply another 4-fold. Then refrigerate for
another 30 min. Repeat this 3 more times.

Freeze the dough to store it.

### Potato filling:

1.  Potatoes (I think I used 2-3 lbs of potatoes this time, which made a lot)
2.  Eggs (4 eggs for the amount of potatoes I used)
3.  Kosher salt and pepper to taste

Peel, boil, and mash the potatoes. Add the eggs and mix in. Then add
salt and pepper to taste.

Bake for 35 min at 350 F.

For 1 lb 15 oz of potatoes, I used 2 eggs and 2 tsp of salt, and  1 tsp
of pepper. 2 eggs was plenty, possibly more than enough, the mashed
potatoes turned out a bit yellow and very creamy.

I made rectangular shapes for the bourekas, which were 3-4 inches in
width. [I should probably 3 inches since 4 inches was quite
large.]{style="color: red"}

1 egg + 1/4 cup of water for the egg wash.

## Spinach and cheese bourekas
### For spinach and cheese bourekas, the filling is:

-   3 cups cooked spinach
-   1/2 cup ricotta
-   1 cup feta
-   3 eggs (this turned out very liquidy, can probably go with
    fewer)
-   1 tsp of ground pepper

Cut the dough into 3.5 inch squares and place about 1.5-2 tsp of filling
in the middle (with 3 eggs its runny, which makes it harder to
fold). Fold into a triangle, sealing the filling in
the middle. Bake at 350 F for 35 min.

The feta cheese and ricotta cheese was already salty, so I didn't need to add more salt.
