---
title: Sauteed spiced beets
---
*Inspired by a recipe from someone I met at a shared AirBnB.*

### Ingredients:

- Beets, whole, peeled and boiled, then sliced into thin strips
- Oil
- Garlic, crushed
- Onions, diced
- Garam masala
- Tumeric
- Salt & pepper

### Method:

1. Sautee the garlic in oil for a minute, then add the onions. Sautee until translucent
2. Add the beets, salt, and pepper, and cook for 5-10 minutes, until the beets soften.
3. Add the spices and cook for a few more minutes.
