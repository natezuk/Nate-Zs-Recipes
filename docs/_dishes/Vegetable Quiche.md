---
title: Vegetable Quiche
---
*This is based on Sharon's friend's recipe for "Spring Clafoutis", but I have simplified it and modified it so it's a general-purpose vegetable quiche.*

### Ingredients:

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 200-300 g peas
- 200 g other vegetables (broccoli, carrots, leeks, etc.)
- 1 tsp salt
- ~1/2 tsp ground pepper
- 4 eggs
- 125 mL milk
- 125 mL double cream (15% cream)
- 125 g plain flour
- 4 spring onions, finely sliced
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 125 g goat cheese, chopped or crumbled into smaller pieces

### Method:

Preheat the oven to 220 C.

1. Heat a large pan with the oil on medium. Then add the peas, other vegetables, salt, and pepper, and sautee until the vegetables soften and start to brown.
2. Pour the vegetables into a baking tray.
3. In a medium sized bowl, beat the eggs, then mix in the milk and double cream. Add the flour and continue mixing until roughly homogeneous. Then add the spring onions, nutmeg, and goat cheese and mix well.
4. Pour the batter over the vegetables and use a spatula to spread the batter and the vegetables evenly around the pan.
5. Bake at 220 C for 40 minutes.
