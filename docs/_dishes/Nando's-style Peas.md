---
title: Nando's-style Peas
---
*Based on a recipe by the Daises & Pie blog:* https://daisiesandpie.co.uk/how-to-make-nandos-style-peas/

Makes about 6 servings.
10 minutes preparation (once the peas are cooked).

### Ingredients:

- 400 g peas (cooked)
- 50 g olive oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Mint leaves from 2-3 sprigs, finely chopped
- 1 bunch flat leaf parsley, finely chopped (comes to about 2 Tbsp when chopped)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Ground pepper

### Method:

*Make this while the peas are hot.*

1. Pour half the peas into a small- or medium-sized bowl. Mash the peas a bit with a large spoon or a potato masher, so that the peas are open but not thoroughly mashed.
2. Add the rest of the peas, red pepper, mint, parsley, salt, and ground pepper, and mix until everything is combined.
