---
title: Huevos Haminados
---
*A sephardic recipe. It can be found here, but I have doubled it: https://www.food.com/recipe/huevos-haminados-sephardic-jewish-style-eggs-317802*

### Ingredients:

- 12 eggs
- Onion skins from 5-6 onions (at least)
- 1 tsp ground coffee
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vinegar
### Method:

1. Add all ingredients to a pot. Bring to a simmer and cook for at least 5 hours.
