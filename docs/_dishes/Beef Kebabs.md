---
title: Beef Kebabs
---
*Based on the recipe for Kofta by Ottolenghi from his Jerusalem cookbook: https://noseychef.com/2019/12/11/kofta-bsiniyah-ottolenghi/*

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg ground beef (*higher fat is better, kebabs stick together more easily*)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup parsley
- 1 Tbsp baharat spice OR
	- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
	- 1 1/2 tsp allspice
	- 3/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper

### Method:

1. Mix together all ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Using your hands, take golf-ball-size scoops of the meat mix and mold into an oblong shape. Place on a large plate.
3. Repeat 2 until all of the meat mix has been used. Place the plate of kebabs in the fridge until you plan to cook them.
4. Heat the oven to 200 C. Meanwhile, heat a large pan with oil on medium-high heat.
5. Place the kebabs in the pan to brown each side (3-ish minutes cooking time), rotating with a spatula to brown all sides. When browned on all sides, place in a large baking dish.
	- *I have had to do this in two rounds in order to cook all of the kebabs*
6. Bake the kebabs in the oven for 10 minutes until cooked through.
