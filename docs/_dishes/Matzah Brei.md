---
title: Matzah Brei
---
*Serves 2*

### Ingredients:

- 3 matzah, broken into smaller pieces
- 4 eggs
- 100 mL milk
- Pinch salt

### Method:

1. In a small bowl, beat the eggs, then mix with milk.
2. In a larger bowl, add the matzah and coat with the egg mixture. Mix until the matzah is fully coated. Let sit for a couple of minutes.
3. Fry the matzah bry on medium-low heat, continuously mixing and flipping to make the egg cook on the matzah (instead of running and cooking on its own). About 5 minutes.
