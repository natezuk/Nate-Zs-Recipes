---
title: Latkes
---

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg potato, with skins, grated
- 2 large onions, peeled and sliced into thin slices
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground pepper
- 2 tsp baking soda
- Canola or vegetable oil

### Method:

1. Combine the potato and onions and mix together. Add the beaten eggs and mix well. Then add the flour, salt, pepper, and baking soda. Mix until fully incorporated.
2. Heat oil in a large pan on medium-high, enough to fully coat the pan in a layer.
3. Using a large spoon and a spatula (or two large spoons), scoop the potato mix, flatten, and place in the hot oil. Do this in batches of 3-4, depending on the size of the pan. Heat for a few minutes on each side, then transfer to a plate covered with a paper towel.
4. Repeat step 3 for the rest of the potato mix.
