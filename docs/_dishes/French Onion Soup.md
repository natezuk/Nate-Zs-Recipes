---
title: French Onion Soup
---
### Ingredients:

- 3 large onions, sliced
- 100 g olive oil (or use 100 g butter and 2 Tbsp olive oil)
- 1/4 cup red wine
- 1.5 L chicken stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp salt
- Ground pepper

### Method:

1. Heat oil (and butter if using) in a medium-sized pot (should hold at least 2.5 L). Add the onions and heat until they turn translucent. *Do not heat the onions long enough to turn brown.*
2. Add the red wine and mix into the onions, cook for about a minute. Then add the stock, bay leaves, thyme, salt, and ground pepper.
3. Bring to a simmer and let simmer for at least 30 minutes.

### Baking and serving:

- On a small slice of bread, add: 3 thin slices of gruyere or swiss cheese and 2 Tbsp parmesan or grana pradano grated cheese.
- Pour the soup into a oven-proof bowl and place the bread and cheese on top of the soup.
- Bake at 175 C for 15 minutes
	- *Alternatively, you can bake the bread and cheese separately, then place on the soup. Especially if you don't have an oven-safe bowl.*

---
### Notes:

*(29-3-2023)* 1 tsp might not be enough salt for the soup on its own, but the cheese with the bread slice usually adds some saltiness.
