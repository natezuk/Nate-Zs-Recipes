---
title: Lomo Saltado
---
*A Peruvian beef stir fry. Based on the recipe here: https://tasty.co/recipe/peruvian-lomo-saltado*

### Ingredients:

- Vegetable oil
- 1 kg sirloin steak, cut into thin strips
- 2 red onions, sliced
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup dark soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp white vinegar
- Large bunch cilantro, finely chopped (*or parsley*)

### Method:

1. Sautee the steak in a large pan until it browned. Remove from pan.
2. Add the onions, peppers, and garlic to the pan. Cook until they soften and the onions become translucent.
3. Add the soy sauce and vinegar and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the steak and the cilantro to the pan. Mix in with the sauce. Cook until the steak is fully cooked through.
