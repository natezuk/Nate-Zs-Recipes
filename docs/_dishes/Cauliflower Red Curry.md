---
title: Cauliflower Red Curry
---
### Ingredients:

- 1 whole cauliflower, chopped
- 2 red peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 5-6 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1-2 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp merquenne (*this is a smokey spice, it can be substituted with something else or skipped*)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin

### Method:

1. Saute the cauliflower in a large pan with oil, and add salt and 1/2 the paprika when adding it to the pan.
2. Add peppers, onion, and garlic. Saute until they start to become translucent, then add the rest of the spices.
3. Saute for a few minutes then add 1 Tbsp of water to steam the veggies. Saute until the cauliflower is soft.
