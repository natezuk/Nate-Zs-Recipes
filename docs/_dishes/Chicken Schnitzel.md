---
title: Chicken Schnitzel
---

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg chicken breasts, smashed so it is thin (less than 1 cm)
- 200 g bread crumbs (unsalted is best)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 - 1 1/2 Tbsp paprika
- 1 tsp ground pepper
- 1/4 cup sesame seeds
- 2 eggs
- 400 mL water

### Method:

1. In a medium sized bowl, combine the bread crumbs, salt, paprika, pepper, and sesame seeds. Mix until homogeneous.
2. In a small bowl, beat the eggs using a fork. Add the water and mix well.
3. For each chicken breast:
	1. With one hand, add the chicken to the egg mixture. Completely coat by flipping in the mix a few times.
	2. Using the same hand, place the chicken in the dry ingredient mix and press into the mix to coat the underside of the chicken breast.
	3. Using the *other* hand, sprinkle some of the dry mix on top of the upper surface of the chicken breast. Then flip the chicken over.
	4. Using the same hand as 3, continue flipping the breast until it is completely coated in the dry mix.
	5. Place the chicken breast on a plate.
4. Prepare a large pan with a layer of oil so it completely coats the bottom. Heat to medium heat.
5. Fry the chicken schnitzel, at least twice on each side for 1-2 minutes. When the schnitzel is cooked through, you will see the schnitzel form moisture on the upper side away from the oil.
