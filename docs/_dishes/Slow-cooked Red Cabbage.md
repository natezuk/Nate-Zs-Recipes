---
title: Slow-cooked Red Cabbage
---
*Thanks Sharon! Based on her recipe.*

### Ingredients:

- 1/2 head of red cabbage, sliced into strips
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 500 mL chicken stock
- 150 mL apple vinegar
- 250 mL water
- 3/4 cup packed dark brown sugar

### Method:

1. Sautee the red cabbage with the oil and salt in a large pan.
2. Add to cabbage to a large pot or slow cooker. Add the sugar, stock, water, and vinegar.
3. In a pot, bring to a simmer, then cook for at least two hours. In a slow cooker, cook on high for 4 hours, or low for 8.
