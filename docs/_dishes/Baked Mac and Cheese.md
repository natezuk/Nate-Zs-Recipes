---
title: Baked Mac & Cheese
---

### Ingredients:

- 500 g pasta
- 5-6 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp green peppercorns
- 30 g olive oil (3 Tbsp)
- 3 Tbsp flour
- 500 mL milk
- 400 g cheese slices (16 slices), yellow, cheddar, or american cheese
- ~1 tsp ground black pepper
