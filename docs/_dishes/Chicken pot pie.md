---
title: Chicken Pot Pie
---
_This is a good use of the chicken and veggies cooked when making chicken stock. I mix the veggies up often, with less carrots, no chicken breast, add potatoes, add peas and other frozen veggies, etc. But the rest of the ingredients are the same._

### Ingredients:

-   Veggies from chicken stock (500 - 1000 g?)
    -   AND/OR 1 kg chicken beasts, cubed
    -   AND/OR 250 g potatoes, cubed
    -   AND/OR 250 - 500 g frozen veggies
-   1/3 cup olive oil + 1/4 cup olive oil
-   1/2 cup plain flour
-   1 tsp salt
-   500 ml stock
-   500 ml water

### Method:

1.  Fry the chicken / stock veggies / potatoes / frozen veggies in a pot with 1/3 cup olive oil and the salt. (If using chicken breasts, add the chicken breasts first and fry until lightly cooked on each side.) Fry for about 5 minutes.
2.  Separately, add 1/4 cup olive oil to a small pot and heat on medium low heat until hot. Add flour and continuously stir until it becomes a homogeneous paste (a “roux”).
3.  Add the roux to the chicken and veggies in the larger pot and mix in. Then add the liquids. When the liquids reach a simmer, lower the heat and cook for another 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the broth is thick and bubbling.
	-   This is nice with puff pastry! When using puff pastry, either:
	    -  Pour the soup into an oven-safe bowl and top the bowl with puff pastry. Put in oven as soon as possible and bake at 175 C for 20 minutes.
	    - Bake the puff pastry separately on a tray with aluminum foil, at 175 C for 15 minutes.
