---
title: Food Wars Fried Chicken
---
*This is a slightly modified recipe of the fried chicken from the manga Food Wars.*

See step 1 -- This should be done several hours before frying the chicken.

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized chunks (about 1 inch long)
- 1 small apple, with skin, sliced
- 1/2 large onion, peeled and chopped
- 4 Tbsp chicken stock
- 4 Tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 Tbsp dark brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- about 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- Corn flour or potato starch

### Method:

1. Puree all of the ingredients except the chicken breasts and starch. Put the chicken in a large ziploc bag and pour the puree over the chicken. Refrigerate for several hours to marinade the chicken.
2. Heat a large pan with oil on medium heat.
3. Pour about 1 1/2 cups of starch into a bowl. With one hand, put the marinaded chicken into the bowl. Use the other hand to toss the chicken and put in the pan with oil.
4. Fry the chicken for 5 minutes, flip after 2 minutes
5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 until all of the chicken is fried.

---
### Notes:

* I thought maybe there was too much vinegar in the marinade, but we liked the flavor overall (Jasmine called it "fruity")
* Adding honey might be good to sweeten the marinade.
