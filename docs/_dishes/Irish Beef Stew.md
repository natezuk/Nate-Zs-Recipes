---
title: Irish Beef Stew
---
*Based on a recipe from the blog Dinner at the Zoo:* https://www.dinneratthezoo.com/irish-stew-recipe/

About 1 hour preparation + 2-3 hours cooking time.
Makes 8-10 servings.

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg beef, cubed
- 2 large onions, peeled and coarsely chopped
- 4 large carrots, peeled and chopped into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 bulb garlic, minced
- 1 kg potatoes, cubed
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper
- 1/4 cup plain flour
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 250 mL dark beer (preferably a stout, like Guinness)
- 1 L water or stock
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 2 Tbsp parsley, finely chopped

### Method:

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot on medium heat. Add the beef and brown on all sides and the fat starts to melt and bubble (about 5 minutes). Remove the beef from the pot.
2. Add the onions, carrots, and garlic. Cook until the onions start to get translucent (about 5 minutes).
3. Return the beef to the pot, then add the flour and coat all of the beef and veggies. Cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add the potatoes, tomato paste, beer, water, thyme, and parsley. Bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Cook for 2-3 hours, until the beef is tender (the potatoes should definitely be cooked at this point).

---
### Notes:

- This can also be cooked in a slow cooker for a longer period of time.
