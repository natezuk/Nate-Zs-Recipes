---
title: Kettle Corn
---
*I got this recipe from Ezra and Agnes. I believe it is still their recipe, though my dad also gave me some advice, and might have modified it slightly.*

### Ingredients:

- 1/2 cup corn kernels
- 1/4 cup sunflower oil or vegetable oil (*Only use oils with a high smoke point, not canola oil or olive oil*)
- 1/4 cup sugar
- Salt and pepper

### Method:

1. Heat the oil and two corn kernels in large pot, covered with a lid, on high heat. Heat until both corn kernels pop.
2. Add the corn kernels and sugar. Cover with a lid immediately.
3. Shake the pot continuously, stopping only for a few seconds at a time to check on the corn kernels popping. Because the pot gets hot, hold the pot with oven mits or pot holders.
4. The rate of popping should speed up pretty quickly over the next 30 seconds. When the rate slows down again and you hear one pop every two seconds or more, then the popcorn is ready.
5. Immediately pour into a large bowl. Sprinkle with plenty of salt and ground pepper, and mix well.
