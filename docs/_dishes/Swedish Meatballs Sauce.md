title: Swedish Meatballs Sauce
---
*Based on the Ikea recipe found here: [https://globalnews.ca/news/6846188/ikea-meatball-recipe-coronavirus/](https://globalnews.ca/news/6846188/ikea-meatball-recipe-coronavirus/).*

Takes 10-15 minutes to make.

### Ingredients:

-   40 grams of oil
-   40 grams plain flour
-   300 mL warm water
-   150 of thick double cream (*I used 15% cream*)
-   2 teaspoons of soy sauce
-   1 teaspoon of Dijon mustard

### Method:

Cook the meatballs either beforehand or during the sauce..

1. Heat the oil in a small pot on medium heat. When the oil is hot, add the flour (it should bubble at bit immediately after putting it in). Mix the flour until it dissolves in the oil. Cook for a few minutes.
2. Add the water and stir continuously until the sauce thickens, after a few minutes of stirring. It will thicken pretty suddenly, like a thick gravy.
3. Add the cream, soy sauce, and mustard, and mix until incorproated.
4. Add the meatballs and cover with the sauce.

## Meatballs and gravy

*(25-11-2025) Updated variant, based on Hello Fresh recipe*
*Meatballs take 20 minutes to cook. Gravy takes about 10 minutes.*
### Ingredients:

*Meatballs:*
- 500 g minced meat
- 1 small apple, grated
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 20 g breadcrumbs
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp dried rosemary

*Gravy:*
- 45 grams olive oil
- 1/4 cup plain flour
- 1 gel packet chicken stock
- 500 mL boiling water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar

#### Method:

1. Combine the meatball ingredients in a large bowl. Mix well.
2. Roll the meatball mix into balls roughly golf ball sized. Place on a plate.
3. Bake the meatballs at 200 C for 20 minutes.
4. While the meatballs are cooking, heat the oil for the gravy in a small saucepan on medium heat. *(start about 10 minutes into cooking the meatballs)*
5. Once the oil is heated, add the flour and salt. Mix until a roux forms and it bubbles a bit.
6. Add the vinegar and cook for about 30 seconds.
7. Then add the chicken stock (cube or packet) and boiling water. Mix until the stock dissolves.
8. Turn the heat to low. Stir the gravy occasionally until the meatballs are ready.
