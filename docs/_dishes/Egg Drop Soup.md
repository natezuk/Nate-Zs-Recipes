---
title: Egg Drop Soup
---
*Makes 2 servings.*

### Ingredients:

- 2 cups chicken stock
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 eggs

### Method:

1. Heat the stock, soy sauce, sesame oil in a small pot.
2. While the soup is heating, beat the eggs in a small bowl.
3. Once the soup is boiling, turn the heat to low. Pour the eggs into the soup while continuously stirring. Cook for about 30 seconds, until the eggs have turned white and the soup is cloudy.
