---
title: Mulled Beer
---

### Ingredients:

- 1 L red ale (a ligher ale is ok too, but not too hoppy)
- 2 fl oz lemon juice (1 1/2 lemons)
- Lemon peels from 1 lemon
- 11 cloves
- 2 star anise
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4 Tbsp sugar
- 4 Tbsp honey

### Method:

Heat all of the ingredients in a pot until it is steaming, no longer foamy, but don't boil. Keep on low heat for 10 minutes.
