---
title: Hot Whisky
---
*The traditional hot whisky is just cloves and lemon, but I liked it with a cinnamon stick as well.*

### Ingredients:

- 1.5 fl oz whisky (ideally Irish whisky, not peaty)
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 slice lemon
- 7-8 cloves
- 1 half cinnamon stick
- Boiling water

### Method:

Stick the cloves in each of the segments of the lemon slice. Place the lemon in the bottom of a mug, and add the sugar. Muddle the sugar into the lemon but pushing it into the slice with a spoon. Add the cinnamon stick, and then add the whisky. Pour in the boiling water to fill the mug.
