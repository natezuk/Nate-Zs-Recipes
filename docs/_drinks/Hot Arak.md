---
title: Hot Arak
---
*I have listed two different versions of "Hot Arak".*

## Mint

### Ingredients:

- 1-2 tsp white sugar
- Bunch of mint leaves
- 1.5 fl oz arak
- Boiling water

### Method:

Add the sugar and mint to a mug. Muddle the mint into the sugar using a spoon. Add the arak, then add the boiling water until the mug is full.

---
## Cinnamon

### Ingredients:

- 1.5 fl oz arak
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 cardamom pods
- 1/2 cinnamon stick
- Boiling water

### Method:

Add the sugar, cardamom, and cinnamon stick to a mug. Add the arak, then add the boiling water until the mug is full.
