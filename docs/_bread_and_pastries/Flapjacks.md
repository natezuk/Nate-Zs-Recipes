### Ingredients:

- 250 g oats
- 200 g olive oil
- 200 g demarera sugar
- 5 Tbsp silan
- 1/2 cup peanut butter

### Method:

1. Combine oil, sugar, silan, and peanut butter in a small bowl. Mix well until homogeneous.
2. Add to the oats in a larger bowl and mix until fully incorporated.
3. Pour into a baking sheet about 8 x 6 inches with parchment paper.
4. Bake at 175 C for 20 minutes.
5. Let cool in the pan for 30 minutes. Then cover with plastic wrap and let cool in the refrigerator until hardened.
