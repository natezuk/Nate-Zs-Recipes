title: Pancakes
---
*I think this recipe is an amalgamation of several, including the recipe from Lateral Cooking and Baking and Pastry.*

Makes 6-7 pancakes.

### Ingredients:

- 250 g plain flour
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 10 fl oz milk (can be mixed/substituted with buttermilk or yogurt, which add more flavor)

### Method:

1. Combine the dry ingredients in a medium size bowl, and mix until homogeneous.
2. In a smaller bowl, mix the eggs so the yolks and whites are mixed in, then add the milk. Mix until homogenous.
3. Heat a pan on medium heat with a thin layer of oil. As the pan is heating, mix the liquid ingredients into the bowl of dry ingredients. Mix until fully incorproated, but don't overmix; the liquids should be fully mixed in, and the batter should thicken, but there will be some small clumps of flour still in the mixture.
4. Use a ladle to add about 1/2 cup batter for each pancake. Fry until the batter starts to bubble (about 1 minute), then flip and fry on the other side for another minute. Remove and place on a large plate lined with a paper towel (to soak up grease).
5. Repeat step 4 for the rest of the pancakes.
