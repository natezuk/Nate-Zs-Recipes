---
title: Focaccia
---
### Ingredients:

- 700 g bread flour
- 420 mL warm water
- 9 g instant dry yeast
- 14 g salt
- 50 g olive oil (+ more for spreading)
- Herb mix (parsley, oregano, thyme, basil)

### Method:

1. Combine all ingredients, mix, then knead until it forms a tight dough.
2. Let rise for 2 hours.
3. Press the dough into a well-greased 9x5 inch pan with olive oil, spreading the dough so it is even across the pan. Pour over olive oil and herbs to coat the bread.
4. Let the dough sit for 30 minutes while the oven is preheating.
5. Bake at 200 C for 25-30 minutes.

---
### Notes

*(10-2-2024)* If using a stand mixer, reduce the amount of water to about 350-375 mL.
