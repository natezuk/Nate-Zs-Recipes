---
title: Brioche
---
*Based on Lateral Cooking.*

Time: ~40 min prep + 2 hrs waiting + 5-10 minutes prep + 30 min waiting + 40 min baking

### Ingredients:

- 700 g bread flour
- 9 g instant yeast
- 4 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 5 eggs
- 150 ml water
- 250 g butter (softened)

### Method:

1. Combine the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl and mix. Then mix the eggs in a small bowl until homogeneous and add to the flour mixture, stirring while adding.
2. Add the water and mix well until the dough is a cohesive mass throughout and there is no dry flour left in the bowl.
3. Lay the dough out in a floured surface and knead for about a minute until it forms a dense dough.
4. Add 50 g butter and knead into the dough.
5. Repeat step 4 until all of the butter has been added to the dough through kneading. The dough will get sticky with each addition, so you will probably need to add more flour to the floured surface.
6. Continue kneading until the dough is stretch and not as sticky. When you make an indentation with your fingers, it will spring back more than at the start, but not as much as usual bread dough. This takes about 10 more minutes of kneading.
7. Place the dough in a greased bowl and let rise for 1.5-2 hours. (*If the dough still seems sticky and doesn't spring back after kneading  in step 6, I have done additional kneading after 30 minutes in the bowl for about 5-10 seconds and this seems to help*).
8. Lay the dough out on a lightly floured surface and split the dough into thirds. [Shape each third into a boule](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWA0RAAsBHg). Cover with a towel and let rest for about 5 minutes.
9. Shape each third into a boule again to tighten the dough, then place each boule side by side in a greased bread pan. Cover with a towel and let rest for 20-30 minutes. (*It will start to rise in the pan*)
10. Bake the bread at 175 C for 40 minutes, until it reaches an internal temperature of 85 C.
