---
title: Matza
---
*This is not strictly kosher, but I needed to come up with a recipe during lockdown :-P*

Takes about 2 hours and makes 14 matzos.

### Ingredients:

- 1 kg bread flour (or 700 g bread flour and 300 g whole wheat flour)
- 20 g salt
- 600 mL cold water

### Method:

Preheat the oven to 250 C.

1. Mix the ingredients together until it forms a dough.

*For each 90 g piece of dough:*
2. Take a 90 g piece of dough and roll into an 8 in x 8 in square (use a dough cutter to cut into a square shape if there is excess). Use a fork to poke holes in the dough in rows 1 cm apart.
3. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 12 minutes.
