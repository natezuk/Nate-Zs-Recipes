\textcolor{green}{(11/7/2017)}

Recipe from \textit{Baking \& Pastry}, p. 310.

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item All-purpose flour = 15 oz
\item Whole wheat flour = 6 oz
\item Baking soda = 0.3 oz
\item Sugar = 12 oz
\item Butter = 6 oz
\item Molasses = 85 g
\item Allspice = 1/4 tsp
\item Cinnamon = 1/4 tsp
\item Salt = a pinch
\item Eggs = 2
\item Water = 75 mL
\item Dried cranberries = 8.1 oz
\end{enumerate}

Cream butter and sugar.  Add molasses and mix in.  Whisk together eggs and water, then beat into the mixture. Add allspice and cinnamon and mix. Separately, combine flour and baking soda and add to the butter/sugar mixture in 3 batches, mixing with a fork.  Add cranberries and mix in.

Scoop each cookie onto a sheet pan with parchment paper. Bake at 375 F for 15 min. \textcolor{red}{(I started with 10 min as in the cookbook but that was too little, I think because the cookbook makes smaller cookies.  15 min cooks them thoroughly enough.)}

\textbf{Servings: 25 cookies}
