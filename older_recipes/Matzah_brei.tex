\textcolor{green}{(4/2/2018)}

Enough for 2-3 servings (~200 g each).

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{itemize}
\item Matzah = 4 sheets
\item Eggs = 4
\item Water = 1 cup
\item Salt = pinch
\item Sugar = 1 tsp
\end{itemize}

Break up matzah into a big bowl.  Separately whisk eggs and add water, stirring until homogoneous.  Mix the egg mixture into the matzah to completely coat it.  Sprinkle in the salt and sugar and mix.  Let sit for ~3 minutes to let the liquid soak into the matzah.  Heat butter in a large pan and fry all at once on medium heat.

\textcolor{blue}{I thought this would be too much liquid (note I used extra large eggs this time), but the egg mixture soaked pretty well into the matzah.  I cooked this until it was just underdone, like I do with scrambled eggs, because I knew it would continue cooking on the pan.  However, I think it wasn't enough because it didn't come out browned at all.  Perhaps a higher heat would be better too.}
