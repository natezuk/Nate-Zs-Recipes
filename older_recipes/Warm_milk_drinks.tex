\textcolor{blue}{I searched for warm milk drinks that are alternatives to milk and honey.  This one is a good alternative.}

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Milk = 12 fl oz
\item Sugar = 1 Tbsp
\item Vanilla = 1/2 tsp
\item Cinnamon = dash
\end{enumerate}

Head milk in a mug in the microwave for ~1 min 30 sec, then mix in the other ingredients.
