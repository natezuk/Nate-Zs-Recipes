\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{itemize}
\item Mustard powder = 1 Tbsp
\item White vinegar = 1 tsp
\item Water = 1 Tbsp
\item Salt = 1/2 tsp
\item Sugar = 1 1/2 tsp
\end{itemize}

Stir ingredients together until lumps in the mustard powder and sugar are removed.

\textcolor{blue}{It's very spicy.  The sugar was necessary to keep the mustard from tasting bitter.}

\textcolor{blue}{The mustard is a bit liquidy.  I think I can go with a bit less liquid.  Other recipes did not call for vinegar, but I think it added to the taste or spiciness.  It is hard to taste with the mustard too.}

\textcolor{blue}{I also had added a pinch of paprika.  I'm not sure this is necessary.}
