\textcolor{green}{Based on recipe from Baking \& Pastry, p. 126, but only ~1/4 of the original recipe.}

Makes 2 loaves in 9x5" Pullman pans.

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Bread flour = 1.30 kg \textcolor{red}{(substitute 159 g )}
\item Rye flour = 405 g
\item Yeast = 11 g
\item Water = 1.1 L
\item Sugar = 32 g
\item Salt = 37.25 g
\item Vegetable oil = 32 g \textcolor{red}{(37 g)}
\item Molasses = 32 g
\end{enumerate}

Let yeast bloom for ~5 min with water and ~300 g of flour.  Then mix all of the ingredients together and knead the dough until tight.  Let rise for ~1.5-2 hrs.  Split in half, shape into a round, let rest for 15 min, then shape again and place in a Pullman pan.  Let rise for ~1 hr. Bake at 400 F for 35 min.

This recipe make two loaves that weighed 1.47 kg and 1.39 kg.

\textbf{Serving size is 110-140 g.}
