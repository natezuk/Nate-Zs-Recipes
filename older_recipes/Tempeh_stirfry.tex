\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Cauliflower = 834 g
\item Green pepper = 469 g
\item Yellow onion = 427 g
\item Shiitake mushrooms = 119 g
\item Carrots = 169 g
\item Garlic = 25 g
\item Bok choy = 617 g
\item Tempeh (lightlife flax seed) = 8 oz
\item Eggs = 4
\item Chicken stock = 1/2 cup
\item Peanut oil = 1 Tbsp
\item Brown rice (cooked) ~ 6 cups
\end{enumerate}

I also used green curry paste, chili powder, and a decent amount of soy sauce.

\textbf{Servings:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item 477 g (12/4/2017)
\item 445 g, 474 g (12/5/2017)
\item 468 g (12/6/2017)
\item 476 g (12/7/2017)
\item 472 g, 468 g (12/9/2017)
\end{enumerate}
