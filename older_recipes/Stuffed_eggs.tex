\textbf{Ingredients:} (amounts from book are listed, actual amounts I used are in parentheses)

\begin{enumerate}
\item Eggs = 7 (I used 4 eggs, which came out to 193 g egg filling)
\item Green onion = 1 Tbsp (used 4 shallots, 39 g)
\item Dill = 1 Tbsp (I used 13 g)
\item Sour cream = 4 Tbsp (3 Tbsp)
\item Bread crumbs = 3 Tbsp (3/4 Tbsp was mixed into the filling, rest for topping)
\item Salt \textcolor{red}{(I used 1/2 tsp, too much)}
\item Pepper
\item Butter = 1.5 Tbsp \textcolor{red}{(I think I can go with 2 Tbsp next time)}
\end{enumerate}

Hard boil the eggs for 10 min.  Immediately cool them in cold water.

Using a paring knife, cut a small 1 cm slice along the length of the egg through the shell.  Then, use a chefs knife to carefully cut along the slice through the egg lengthwise, so as to not crack the egg beyond the midline slice.  Using a spoon, remove the egg yolk and place in a small bowl.  Next, remove the egg white with the spoon.  If the egg was cooked right, the white should come right out of the shell.  If not, scoop it out, being careful to keep the shell and inner membrane intact.

Pour the egg whites and yolks out onto a cutting board and chop fine.  Return to bowl and add the onion, dill, sour cream, salt, pepper, and 1 Tbsp of bread crumbs.

Sprinkle the rest of the bread crumbs onto a plate.  Scoop the filling into the egg shells so it just overfills the shells.  Invert the shells and dip the filling into the bread crumbs, covering the top.  Repeat this will all of the shells.

In a large frying pan, melt the butter over medium-low heat.  Add the stuffed eggs filling side down.  Fry for about 4 min, until the underside is golden brown, and the butter/bread crumb mix around the eggs forms a roux and is frothing.  Remove from the pan and let cool for 2-3 min.
