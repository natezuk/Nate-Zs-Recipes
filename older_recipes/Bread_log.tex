I plan to use this document to log experimental bread recipes that I make and how they turn out.

\textbf{Whole wheat bread} \textcolor{green}{(1/21/2018)}

\textbf{Ingredients:} (for ~1.7 kg of dough)

\begin{enumerate}
\item Whole wheat flour (40\%): 400 g
\item Bread flour (60\%): 600 g
\item Molasses (4\%): 40 g
\item Sugar (4\%): 40 g
\item Salt (2\%): 20 g
\item Yeast (1\%): 10 g
\item Water (60\%): 600 mL
\end{enumerate}

The actual recipe came out to 1.53 kg of dough.

First, I let the yeast proof in 100 g of bread flour and all of the water (warm).  Then I mixed together the rest of the ingredients and kneaded the dough until it was tight.  Let rise in a oiled container for 1.5 hrs.  I preshaped the dough into two oblongs, let rest for 5 min, then cut and rolled the dough into 100 g rolls.  I let the dough rolls rest for ~30 min before baking at 400 F for 20 min.

Made 15 rolls.

I thought having a total of 8% sugar would make the dough fairly sweet.  The dough rose well (it won't rise well with lots of sugar), but it actually isn't too sweet.  The nuttiness from the whole wheat flour is very strong too.

I let the bread bake on parchment paper.  The rolls started to get dark on the bottom and had a tough crust when they came out.  The crust has softened after cooling, but it's still fairly tough.
