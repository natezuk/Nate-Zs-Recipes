\textbf{Vodka Collins}

\textcolor{green}{(Made on 3/13/2018)}

\begin{itemize}
\item Vodka (3 Tbsp = 6 oz)
\item Lemon juice (3 Tbsp)
\item Simple syrup (3 Tbsp)
\item Soda water
\end{itemize}

Serve in a high ball glass.  Add vodka, lemon juice, and simple syrup.  Then add ice to fill the glass and mix briefly.  Fill the  glass with soda water.  \textcolor{red}{(I used grapefruit soda water, which turned out well)}

\textbf{The Ghost of Manhattan}

\textcolor{green}{(Idea I had using Poitin on 5-9-2021)}

\begin{itemize}
\item O'Begley Poitin (1.5 oz) \textcolor{red}{(This poitin tastes a bit bready, so I think it's good for this drink.)}
\item Vanilla simple syrup (2 Tbsp)
\item Orange peel, spritz oil over drink and on sides of glass
\end{itemize}

To make the vanilla simple syrup, combine equal parts (by volume) sugar and water and bring to a simmer in a small pot. Add a vanilla bean and simmer for several minutes. Take the vanilla simple syrup off the burner and let cool in the fridge. Make sure the simple syrup is cool before adding it to the drink.
