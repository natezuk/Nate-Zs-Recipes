\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Onions = 702 g
\item Peppers = 390 g
\item Mushrooms = 8 oz
\item Jalapenos = 67 g
\item Garlic = 40 g
\item Bok Choy = 645 g
\item Sesame seeds = 1 cup
\item Soy sauce = 1/4 cup
\item Teriaki sauce = 1 cup
\item Green curry paste = 2 Tbsp
\item Peanut oil
\item Rice = 3 cups + 3 cups water (before being cooked)
\item Eggs = 4
\item Chicken stock = 1/2 cup
\end{enumerate}

I marinated the tofu with teriyaki sauce, chili powder, pepper, and some soy sauce.  I sauteed the tofu first separately.  Then I sauteed the vegetables in a large pot, with the curry paste.  then added the tofu and bok choy.  I cracked the eggs and cooked them in the pot with the vegetables before adding the chicken stock.  After that had cooked for 10 min, I added the rice to the pot and mixed everything together.  I added the sesame seeds at the end.

\textcolor{red}{The flavor was good overall, but the sesame seeds had were too crunchy and didn't match the mouthfeel of the rest.  Because of that I don't think they were a good addition.  You couldn't get the flavor of the sesame seeds either.}
