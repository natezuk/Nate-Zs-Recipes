\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Water = 3 cups
\item Tea = 1 Tbsp
\item Ginger = 1 tsp
\item Cardamom = 10 pods
\item Milk = 1 cup
\item Sugar = 3 Tbsp
\end{enumerate}

Boil water with tea leaves, ginger, and cardomom pods.  After boiling for a few minutes, turn the heat down to low and add milk.  Mix for about 2 minutes.  Then turn the heat off and add sugar, and mix until the sugar is dissolved.  Strain the liquid.

\textcolor{red}{I used 2 tsp ginger which turned out to be too spicy.}
