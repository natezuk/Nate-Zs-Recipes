Based on the recipe in \textit{Baking and Pastry}, p. 343.  I've used 1/3 of the recipe.

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{itemize}
\item Peanut butter = 255 g
\item All-purpose flour = 171 g \textcolor{red}{(added another 99 g to thicken the batter)}
\item Baking soda = 5 g (~ 1 tsp)
\item Sugar = 46 g \textcolor{red}{(should have been 151 g, reduced to compensate for dark brown sugar)}
\item Dark brown sugar = 256 g \textcolor{red}{(should have been 151 g, but added too much by accident)}
\item Butter = 151 g (1 1/3 sticks)
\item Salt = 5 g
\item Eggs = 3 (~76 g)
\end{itemize}

Mixed wet ingredients + salt first, then peanut butter, and then dry ingredients (flour and baking soda).  Baked at 350 F for 14 min.

\textcolor{blue}{Texture is good, similar to the chocolate chip cookies.  The peanut butter is not as strong as I was expecting though, and the cookies taste dry (I think because of the peanut butter).  Some additional filling, like chocolate or some spice, would have been a good addition.

\textbf{Servings:} Made 22 cookies.
