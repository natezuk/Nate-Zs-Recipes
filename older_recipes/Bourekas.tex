The puff pastry comes from Baking \& Pastry, "Butter puff pastry dough", p. 231.  I make the recipe in full and freeze the dough that I don't use.  However, the last time I made bourekas, I used nearly all of the dough.

\textcolor{blue}{Potato bourekas (made 12/17/2017)}

\textbf{Puff pastry:}

Puff pastry dough:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Bread flour = 2 lb
\item All purpose flour = 8 oz
\item Salt = 1 oz
\item Butter = 8 oz
\item Water = 18 fl oz
\end{enumerate}

Puff pastry roll-in:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Butter (pliable) = 2 lb 4 oz
\item Bread flour = 4 oz
\end{enumerate}

Mix the dough together and shape into a rough rectangle.  Wrap and place in refrigerator for 30-60 min. 

Make the roll-in by mixing the butter and flour, first with a sturdy spatula or utensil then with your hands.  Shape into a rectangle, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for 30 min.

Turn out the dough on a lightly floured surface and roll out to a rectangle > 2x the size of the roll-in.  Lock-in the roll-in by sealing the dough around the roll-in, then apply a 4-fold.  Refrigerate for 30-45 min.

Remove from the refrigerator, rotate 90 degrees relative to the original orientation, roll out and apply another 4-fold.  Then refrigerate for another 30 min.  Repeat this 3 more times.

Freeze the dough to store it.

\textbf{Potato filling:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Potatoes \textcolor{red}{(I think I used 2-3 lbs of potatoes this time, which made a lot)}
\item Eggs \textcolor{red}{(4 eggs for the amount of potatoes I used)}
\item Kosher salt and pepper to taste
\end{enumerate}

Peel, boil, and mash the potatoes.  Add the eggs and mix in.  Then add salt and pepper to taste.

\textcolor{green}{6/12/2018}

Bake for 35 min at 350 F.

For 1 lb 15 oz of potatoes, I used 2 eggs and 2 tsp of salt, and ~1 tsp of pepper.  2 eggs was plenty, possibly more than enough, the mashed potatoes turned out a bit yellow and very creamy.

I made rectangular shapes for the bourekas, which were 3-4 inches in width.  \textcolor{red}{I should probably 3 inches since 4 inches was quite large.}

1 egg + 1/4 cup of water for the egg wash.

\textcolor{green}{(6/13/2018)}

For spinach and cheese bourekas, the filling is:
\begin{itemize}
\item 3 cups cooked spinach
\item 1/2 cup ricotta
\item 1 cup feta
\item 3 eggs \textcolor{red}{(this turned out very liquidy, can probably go with fewer)}
\item 1 tsp of ground pepper
\end{itemize}

Cut the dough into 3.5 inch squares and place about 1.5-2 tsp of filling in the middle \textcolor{red}{(with 3 eggs its runny, which makes it harder to fold)}.  Fold into a triangle, sealing the filling in the middle.  Bake at 350 F for 35 min.

\textcolor{blue}{The feta cheese and ricotta cheese was already salty, so I didn't need to add more salt.}
