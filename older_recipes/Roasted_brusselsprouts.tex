Based on the recipe from: \href{http://chefchloe.com/recipes/on-the-side/maple-roasted-brussels-sprouts-with-toasted-hazelnuts.html}{Maple Roasted Brussels Sprouts by Chef Chloe}

\textbf{Ingredients:}

\begin{itemize}
\item Brussels sprouts = 1.5 lbs
\item Olive oil = 1/4 cup
\item Salt = 1 tsp
\item Pepper = 1/4 tsp
\item Maple syrup = 2 Tbsp
\item Hazelnuts, coarsely chopped = 1/2 cup \textcolor{red}{(I used walnuts)}
\end{itemize}

Preheat the oven to 375 F.

Chop stems off the brussels sprouts, then chop them in half.  In a large bowl, pour olive oil, salt, and pepper over the brussels sprouts and mix to coat the sprounts.  Spread evenly over a large baking dish with aluminum foil.  Bake for 15 min, then take the dish out and mix the sprouts around, flipping some of them over to bake them evenly.  Then bake for another 30 min.  Drizzle maple syrup and nuts over the sprouts and spread evenly.  Bake for 10-15 min more or until the sprouts are browning and just starting to dry.
