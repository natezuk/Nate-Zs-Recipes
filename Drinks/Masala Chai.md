### Ingredients

- 3 cups water
- 1 Tbsp black tea leaves, or 4 bags black tea
- 1 tsp ginger
- 10 cardamom pods
- 1 cup milk
- 3 Tbsp sugar

### Method

1. Boil water with tea leaves, ginger, and cardomom pods. After boiling for a few minutes, turn the heat down to low and add milk. Mix for about 2 minutes. Then turn the heat off and add sugar, and mix until the sugar is dissolved. Strain the liquid.

---

### Notes

I used 2 tsp ginger which turned out to be too spicy.
