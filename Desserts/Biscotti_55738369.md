*Based on the recipe from Baking & Pastry.*

### Ingredients: {#Biscotti-Ingredients:}

-   500 g bread flour

-   1/2 Tbsp baking soda

-   6 fl oz egg whites (comes from 5 eggs)

-   300 g white sugar

-   3 g salt (\~1/2 tsp)

-   1 - 1 1/2 Tbsp lemon juice (from 1 squeezed lemon)

-   250 g whole almonds

### Method: {#Biscotti-Method:}

(Preheat oven to 150 C)

1.  In a large bowl, combine the flour and baking soda. Mix well to
    evenly distribute the baking soda.

2.  In a smaller bowl, combine egg whites, sugar, salt, and lemon juice.
    Beat until it lightens in color and thickens.

3.  Add the liquids to the flour and mix well until all of the flour is
    mixed in and it forms a cohesive dough. Then add the almonds and mix
    in.

4.  Shape into a large, wide roll that is about 3-4 inches wide. Place
    on parchment paper on a large baking tray.

5.  Bake at 150 C for 1 hour. Afterwards, let cool for about 15 minutes,
    and turn the oven to 125 C.

6.  Using a bread knife, slice the roll into 1/2 inch thick (\~1 cm)
    slices. Spread them out on a tray (or trays) with parchment paper.

7.  Bake at 125 C for 25 minutes, until golden brown
