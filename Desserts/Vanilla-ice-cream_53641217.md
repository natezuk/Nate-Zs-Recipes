# [ Nate\'s Recipes : Vanilla ice cream ]{#title-text} {#title-heading .pagetitle}
:::

::: {#content .view}
::: page-metadata
Created by [ Nate Zuk]{.author}, last modified on Oct 09, 2022
:::

*Based on recipes from Baking & Pastry and Forgotten Cooking. Variants
are found below.*\
*Note also that this recipe does not involve an ice cream maker because
I don't have one...yet.*

Time: \~40 minutes preparation time + wait overnight + 4 hours mixing
every 30 minutes + 4 hours waiting

### Ingredients: {#Vanillaicecream-Ingredients:}

-   500 ml milk

-   500 ml fresh cream (\~30% cream or double cream)

-   100 g + 100 g sugar (white or golden sugar)

-   1 tsp vanilla

-   2 g salt (\~1/2 tsp)

-   5 egg yolks

### Method: {#Vanillaicecream-Method:}

1.  In a medium-sized pot, mix 100 g sugar with the milk, sugar,
    vanilla, and salt on medium-low heat. Mix occasionally, and heat
    until steaming. (*Do step 2 while this is happening*)

2.  In a small bowl, combine 100 g sugar with the egg yolks. Mix until
    eggs slightly lighten in color.

3.  When the milk and cream from step 1 is heated, **temper** the eggs
    with 1/3 of the cream mixture: Gradually add the cream to the eggs
    (starting with \~1 Tbsp and increasing to a 1/4 cup) and
    continuously mix to evenly distribute the warm liquid in the eggs.
    This slowly raises the temperature of the eggs without cooking them.

4.  Add the tempered eggs into the cream mixture in the pot. Heat on
    low, stirring continuously, until the temperature of the mixture
    reaches 80 C. It should slightly thicken at this temperature
    (becomes a custard).

5.  Immediately place the pot in an ice bath to cool the custard. Once
    at about room temperature, pour into a large bowl and place in the
    fridge to cool overnight.

6.  The next day, whip the custard in the large bowl until it becomes
    foamy, increases in volume by 1.5x, and lightens in color.
    (*Alternatively, if the bowl has a lid, you can shake the bowl to
    mix*)

7.  Pour into a medium sized bowl with a lid. Place in the freezer for
    30 minutes.

8.  After 30 minutes, remove from freezer and stir well to remove the
    liquid freezing to the sides of the bowl and lid. Place back in the
    freezer.

9.  Repeat step 8 until the ice cream is not liquidy and is viscous when
    mixed (you can see swirls as you mix it), about 4-6 hours.

10. Place in the freezer. Freeze for at least 4 hours or overnight
    before serving.

### Variations: {#Vanillaicecream-Variations:}

**Chocolate ice cream**: During step 1, melt 300-350 g of chocolate into
the cream mixture.

**Cinnamon chai ice cream**: During step 1, add 2 large cinnamon sticks,
20 cardamom pods, 2 tsp ginger, and 4 bags black tea. Heat for an extra
10 minutes on low to steep the tea and spices. Remove the tea bags and
cinnamon sticks before proceeding to step 2.

**Chocolate chip ice cream**: During the last mixing in step 9, mix in
250 g chocolate chips.
