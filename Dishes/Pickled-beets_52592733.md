# [ Nate\'s Recipes : Pickled beets ]{#title-text} {#title-heading .pagetitle}

### Ingredients: {#Pickledbeets-Ingredients:}

-   2 large beets, peeled and sliced into chunks

-   1 cup vinegar

-   1/2 cup sugar

-   1 tsp salt

-   1 cup water

### Method: {#Pickledbeets-Method:}

1.  Place beets in a pot and fill with water. Bring to a simmer and
    simmer for 15 minutes.

2.  (*This step can be done during 1*) In a separate pot, combine the
    vinegar, sugar, salt, and 1 cup water. Mix to dissolve the sugar,
    then bring to a boil. Once it starts boiling, turn off the heat and
    let set for a few minutes.

3.  When the beets are cooked, pour the beets (not the beet juice) into
    clean glass jars. Then pour over the pickling liquid made in step 2
    until it completely covers the beets.

4.  Let the beets cool at room temperature for 15 minutes, then place in
    the fridge. Refrigerate overnight before serving.
