### Ingredients: {#Roastedkale-Ingredients:}

-   250 g kale

-   2 Tbsp olive oil

-   2 tsp balsamic vinegar

-   1/4 tsp salt

-   1/4 tsp ground pepper

### Method: {#Roastedkale-Method:}

1.  In a large bowl, toss the kale with the other ingredients.

2.  Spread the kale on parchment paper on a large baking tray.

3.  Bake at 175 C for 10 minutes.

4.  Take the tray out of the oven, and use a spatula to gently move/flip
    the kale. Then bake for another 10 minutes, until dry and crispy
