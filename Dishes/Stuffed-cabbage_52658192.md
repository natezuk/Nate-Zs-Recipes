### Ingredients {#Stuffedcabbage-Ingredients}

-   A whole white cabbage (cut off the base just above the stem to make
    the leaves easier to remove in step 3)

*Filling*: (this is a lot, you will have left-over filling)

-   1/2 cup uncooked rice + 1 cup water \--\> first step is to cook the
    rice. Use all of it in the filling.

-   1 egg

-   1 kg ground beef (2 lbs)

-   1 whole onion, finely chopped

-   3 Tbsp water

*Sauce:*

-   1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes per batch (in the US I used 1 small
    can, \~1.5 cups, per batch

-   1 large onion per batch, sliced

-   2 tsp salt per batch

-   1/2 tsp ground pepper

-   2 Tbsp honey per batch

-   1/4 cup lemon juice per batch

-   1/4 cup sultanas (or raisins) per batch

### Method {#Stuffedcabbage-Method}

1.  Mix together the filling and set aside.

2.  Heat up water in a large pot to a simmer. Place the whole head of
    cabbage in the water and cook for about 7-10 min. By this point, you
    should be able to remove the outermost leaf with tongs. Turn the
    heat to the lowest setting (feel free to turn the heat off during
    the next step if you think the cabbage is cooking too fast and the
    leaves are getting too soft and/or falling apart)

3.  For each leaf, remove the leaf from the base with tongs, and cook in
    the water for another 20 seconds. Remove from the leaf from the
    water, and wrap around a spoonful of filling (I usually fold from
    each side along the stem first, and then roll up perpendicular to
    the stem). Place the stuffed cabbage folded side down in the pot for
    cooking. Repeat for as many leaves as you can remove from the
    cabbage, or until you run out of room in your pot (do not stack the
    stuffed cabbages).

4.  For each batch, pour in the crushed tomatoes, onion, salt, and
    pepper. (Recently, I have also added enough water to just cover the
    stuffed cabbages, if the sauce isn\'t liquidy enough). Cover with a
    lid and cook on medium-low heat (a simmer) for 1.5 hrs.

5.  Add the honey, lemon juice, and sultanas to each batch, and cook for
    another 30 min.
