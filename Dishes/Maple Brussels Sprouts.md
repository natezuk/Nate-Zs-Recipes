*I think this is from a Wegman's booklet of recipes*.

Time: 5 minutes prep (after cutting brussels sprouts) + 25 minutes baking

### Ingredients:

-   500 g brussels sprouts, washed and cut in half
-   2 Tbsp olive oil
-   1 Tbsp maple syrup
-   1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
-   1/2 tsp salt
-   Ground pepper

### Method:

Add all of the ingredients together in a large bowl and mix. Spread evenly on a baking tray with aluminum foil. Bake at 175 C for 15 minutes. Flip the brussels sprouts with a spatula, then bake for another 10 minutes.
