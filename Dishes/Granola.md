### Ingredients: 

-   400 g oats
-   200 g nuts (almonts, hazelnuts, walnuts, pecans)
-   1/2 tsp salt
-   1 tsp cinnamon
-   100 g dark brown sugar
-   100 g olive oil
-   1 tsp vanilla

### Method: 

1.  Combine the dry ingredients in a large bowl and mix well.
2.  Combine the sugar, oil, and vanilla in a smaller bowl and mix until almost evenly mixed (the sugar will not dissolve in the oil).
3.  Pour the sugar and oil mix over the dry ingredients and mix until everything is coated with the sugar mix.
4.  Pour the granola onto a large baking tray with parchment paper and spread evenly over the tray.
5.  Bake at 175 C for 15 minutes. Then remove from the oven and mix with a spatula to keep from sticking to the paper. Bake for another 10 minutes.
6.  Remove from the oven and let cool for at least 45 minutes before serving or storing.
