*The caramel recipe is modified from Chocolates & Candies.*

### Ingredients: {#Caramelapples-Ingredients:}

-   6 oz golden sugar

-   1 tbsp dark brown sugar (*recipe originally called for glucose
    syrup. Probably can also be substituted with corn syrup, honey or
    agave)*

-   100 g butter

-   1/4 cup milk

-   1/4 cup 15% cream

-   2 tsp vanilla

-   8 apples, dried

### Method: {#Caramelapples-Method:}

Before: Place the dried apples on a tray lined with parchment paper

1.  Combine the sugar, syrup, milk, and 1/3 of the butter in a saucepan
    and heat on medium to medium-high. Mix until dissolved.

2.  Raise the heat to medium-high, add another 1/3 of the butter and
    bring the dissolved sugar to a boil. Boil until the temperature
    reaches 113 C, then immediately remove from the heat.

    1.  *At this stage it is pretty much a caramel sauce.*

3.  Add the butter and stir. And the cream and vanilla in small
    additions at a time (*especially if it is cold*) stirring
    constantly. (*The original recipe just says to add these, no mention
    of stirring*)

4.  Bring the pot back to medium-high heat and boil until it reaches
    121 C. Then immediately remove from heat and pour over the apples
    (*while caramel is boiling*).

### Notes: {#Caramelapples-Notes:}

-   I let the caramel cool a bit after removing from heat in step 4
    before pouring over the apples, and this made it come out more
    fudge-y. According to the book, the fudge consistency is produced by
    beating the sugar mixture (aka. constantly stirring) after it has
    reached its highest temperature.

-   It might come out better using some sugar syrup instead of dark
    brown sugar.

-   Jasmine thought the caramel might be too buttery.
