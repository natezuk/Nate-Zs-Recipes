# [ Nate\'s Recipes : Crepes ]{#title-text} {#title-heading .pagetitle}

*Partly based on the Mosswood Cookbook and Lateral Cooking*.

Makes 5-6 crepes. Takes \~1 hour.

### Ingredients: {#Crepes-Ingredients:}

-   250 g flour

-   1/4 tsp salt

-   1 Tbsp sugar

-   2 fl oz oil

-   2 eggs

-   250 ml milk

-   250 ml water

### Method: {#Crepes-Method:}

1.  Mix together the dry ingredients (flour, salt, sugar) in a medium
    sized bowl.

2.  In a smaller bowl, beat the eggs, then add the oil and mix. Add the
    milk and water and mix until homogeneous.

3.  Pour the liquids into the bowl with the flour, and mix until
    combined with no dry flour on the bottom of the bowl (there may
    still be small clumps of flour in the mixture). Then wait for 10
    minutes.

4.  Heat about 1 Tbsp oil in a medium-sized pan on medium heat. When
    heated, use a ladle or 1/2 cup measure to pour the batter into the
    pan. Rotate the pan to spread the batter.

5.  When the batter no longer sticks to the pan (about 1 minute). Flip
    the crepe. Cook for another minute then flip again. Place on a plate
    after another 10 seconds.

6.  Repeat until you have finished the batter.
