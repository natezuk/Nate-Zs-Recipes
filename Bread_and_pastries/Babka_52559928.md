# [ Nate\'s Recipes : Babka ]{#title-text} {#title-heading .pagetitle}

*This recipe comes from the book \"A Treasury in Jewish Baking\" by
Marcy Goldman. The original recipe pretty reliably makes 4 babka, aka a
lot of babka. I have modified it somewhat from the original.*

### Ingredients for dough: {#Babka-Ingredientsfordough:}

-   850 g flour (approximately 6 cups)

-   2 Tbsp yeast

-   [3/4 cups sugar]{.inline-comment-marker
    ref="7112e3af-754c-4860-8c75-371ce328058c"}

-   1 tsp salt

-   2 eggs + 2 egg yolks

-   250 g oil (canola, vegetable, or sunflower)

-   1 tsp lemon juice

-   1 tsp vanilla

-   1 cup water

### Method: {#Babka-Method:}

1.  Combine all of the dry ingredients and mix together in a large bowl.

2.  Combine the eggs, lemon juice, and vanilla in a small bowl, and
    whisk the eggs until the eggs yolks and whites are combined. Pour
    into the dry ingredients and mix until combined.

3.  Add the water 1/2 cup at a time and mix the dough after each
    addition. When the water is fully combined it should form a sticky,
    cohesive dough.

4.  Lay the dough out on a floured surface and knead until the dough is
    tight and no longer sticky (about 10 minutes).

5.  Place the dough in a greased container covered with a towel. Let the
    dough rise for 30 minutes in a warm environment (70 - 90 F or 20 -
    30 C).

6.  After 30 minutes, fold the dough in half, and then fold in half
    again perpendicular to the previous fold (this can be done in the
    bowl). Cover the bowl with the towel and let the dough rise for
    another 1.5 hours, until doubled in size.

*Once the dough is ready, you will start adding the filling, see
below\...*

### Ingredients for chocolate filling: {#Babka-Ingredientsforchocolatefilling:}

-   300 g chocolate chips (the smaller the better)

-   1/2 tsp cinnamon

-   100 g dark brown sugar

-   30 g oil

### Ingredients for cinnamon filling: {#Babka-Ingredientsforcinnamonfilling:}

-   [1 cup dark brown sugar]{.inline-comment-marker
    ref="8531fe8b-f1c6-4f5d-8a38-449ef6148851"}

-   1 Tbsp cinnamon

-   50 g oil

*If you had made the dough for 4 babka, first split the dough into 2
halves.*

### Method (continued): {#Babka-Method(continued):}

-   On a floured surface, roll out the dough into a large (at least 9 x
    12 inches) rectangle.

-   Spread the filling on one side of the dough, covering the side as
    much as possible.

-   Tightly roll up the dough with the filling on the inside of the
    roll, starting from the bottom of the rectangle and rolling to the
    top.

-   Cut the roll in half. Where you made the cut, pinch and fold the
    ends so that the filling is not exposed.

-   Take one roll and slice it down the middle until just before the
    ends. Then, gently twist the dough so that at least a 360 degree
    twist is made between the two ends. Try to keep as much of the
    filling in as possible. Then, place the dough in a greased bread
    tin.

-   Repeat step 5 with the other half of the roll.

-   Bake the babka at 175 C for 40 minutes. After cooking, let the pan
    cool for 15 minutes before removing the babka and serving.
