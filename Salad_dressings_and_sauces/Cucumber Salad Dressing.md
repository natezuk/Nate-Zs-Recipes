*For sliced cucumbers, but can be used with other fresh veggies.*

### Ingredients:

- 1 tsp salt (+ more to put on the cucumbers)
- 2 tsp granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp rice vinegar (mirin)
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tsp soy sauce

### Method:

Mix all of the ingredients together to make the sauce. When preparing the salad, coat the cucumbers and other veggies with 1 Tbsp grapeseed or olive oil + a bit of salt. Then add the sauce and mix to coat everything.
